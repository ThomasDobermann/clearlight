Principal Engineer

John Greenham

John is the principal engineer at Clearlight Mastering. Originally from the UK, he worked in the San Francisco Bay Area before moving to Los Angeles, and today works out of the studio's main room in Hollywood.

His four Grammys came through his mastering of Billie Eilish's records: Album of the Year and Best Engineered Album for When We All Fall Asleep, Where Do We Go?, Record of the Year for “bad guy,” and a second Record of the Year for “everything i wanted.” He has also received Grammy and Latin Grammy nominations and certificates across his career, including three for Los Tigres del Norte's Best Norteño Album winners.

He is a trusted mastering engineer to mixer Mark ‘Spike’ Stent, with whom he works frequently. In 2019 the work outgrew one pair of hands, and he brought his daughter Tess into the room. He has been training her ever since.

Mastering Engineer

Tess Greenham

Tess has worked alongside John — her father and mentor — since 2019. She came in as his assistant when the Billie Eilish records made the studio busier than one person could handle, and learned the job on real releases, in the room, with someone good sitting next to her.

She assisted on records for Billie Eilish, Gracie Abrams, Girl In Red, Tinashe, Nessa Barrett, Slayyyter, Liam Gallagher and Joe Jonas, among others. That apprenticeship trained the skill mastering actually runs on — listening — and let the technical knowledge arrive through experience rather than the other way round. When a pandemic landed in her second year, she had the time to go deep on the technical side too.

Tess has been mastering her own projects since 2021, with a heavy emphasis on analog. Her approach is sensory rather than clinical — she works from how a record feels, not from what the meters say. Her clients include Sure Sure, Trousdale, Pom Pom Squad, Ryn Weaver, Ella Vos, Rosie Carney, Transviolet, Sun June, Kathryn Gallagher, Bennett Coast and Déyyess.

Recognition

At the 62nd Grammy Awards, records mastered in this room took Album of the Year, Record of the Year and Best Engineered Album in a single night — all three categories John was nominated in. Record of the Year came back the following year for “everything i wanted.”

Beyond that, three consecutive Best Norteño Album winners for Los Tigres del Norte were mastered here too. The work spans genres, generations and continents.

Awards matter less than the music does. But they don't hurt.
